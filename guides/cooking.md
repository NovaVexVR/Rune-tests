# Cooking

Cooking allows players to prepare food so it can be eaten to restore Hitpoints or provide other useful effects. It is closely tied to Fishing, Farming, and general gathering of ingredients from shops, monsters, fields, and other activities.

Cooking is generally inexpensive and fast to train compared to many other skills. It is useful for combat, Slayer, quests, bossing, and producing specialty foods with temporary stat boosts.

## Getting Started

Most Cooking training is done by using raw food on a range or fire.

Cooking on a range generally burns food less often than cooking on a fire. The Lumbridge Castle range has an additional burn reduction effect for many low-level foods after completing Cook's Assistant, making it one of the best early training spots.

As your Cooking level increases, you unlock better food and burn food less often. Higher-level food usually gives more experience and heals more, but it may burn often if cooked too early.

## Burning Food

Food can burn while cooking. Burn chance is affected by the food being cooked, the player's Cooking level, the cooking source, and certain equipment.

Ranges are usually safer than fires. Fires are still useful when they are much closer to a bank or when cooking while training Fishing.

Cooking Gauntlets from Family Crest reduce the chance of burning many important fish, including Lobsters, Swordfish, Monkfish and Sharks. They are one of the most useful long-term rewards for Cooking training.

Fish are the primary Cooking training food because they are easy to obtain, stack efficiently in banks, and are commonly gathered while training Fishing.

Buying raw fish is generally the fastest way to train Cooking. Catching your own fish while training Fishing is slower overall, but saves money and provides experience in both skills.

Jug of wine is one of the fastest Cooking training methods because it can be made directly at a bank by using grapes on jugs of water. Although it heals 11 hitpoints, it temporarily lowers your Attack level, making it a poor choice for melee combat. It is more useful for players using Magic or Ranged, or for activities such as Agility training or Thieving, where the Attack reduction is less important.

## Food Categories

### Fish

Fish are the backbone of Cooking training and everyday combat food.

Early fish such as Trout and Salmon are useful for low-level combat and are easy to gather. Shrimp are gathered for free south of Lumbridge by the fishing tutor using the small fishing net lying on the ground there. Lobsters and Swordfish are common mid-level foods. Monkfish, Sharks, and even Sea Turtles and Manta Rays are stronger options for higher-level combat. Sea Turtles and Manta Rays are only obtained from the Fishing Trawler minigame, making them much slower and less consistent to accumulate.

Fish are also useful for raising kittens after Gertrude's Cat. Raw or cooked fish can be used to feed kittens, and grown cats can later be exchanged for 100 death runes in West Ardougne.

### Bread, Meat and Cakes

Bread and cooked meat are simple early foods. They are not major long-term training methods, but they are easy to make and appear in several quests and recipes.

Cakes and Chocolate Cakes are eaten in multiple bites, making them useful for low-level combat, questing, and situations where small repeated healing is helpful. Some items in this category, such as 10 pieces of regular bread and a Chocolate Cake, are required for certain quests.

### Pies

Pies are made with pastry dough and a pie dish, then filled and baked. Most pies are eaten in two bites.

Early pies such as Redberry Pie, Meat Pie and Apple Pie are simple healing foods, although Redberry Pie is used for quests. Higher-level pies are more important because they can provide temporary skill boosts.

Useful pies include:

- Garden Pie

- Fish Pie

- Admiral Pie

- Wild Pie

- Summer Pie

Summer Pie is especially useful because it restores run energy and temporarily boosts Agility, making it helpful for Agility training and shortcuts.

### Pizzas

Pizzas are made from a pizza base, tomato and cheese, then baked. Toppings are added after the pizza is cooked.

Common pizzas include:

- Plain Pizza

- Meat Pizza

- Anchovy Pizza

- Pineapple Pizza

Pizzas are eaten in two bites. This makes them useful when a player wants to split healing across two actions rather than using the whole food at once.

Pineapple Pizza is one of the strongest commonly used two-bite foods, with each bite healing 11 HP for a total of 22.

### Potatoes and Dairy

Potatoes become much stronger when combined with dairy products and toppings.

Common potato foods include:

- Baked Potato

- Potato with Butter

- Potato with Cheese

- Chilli Potato

- Egg Potato

- Mushroom Potato

- Tuna Potato

Tuna Potatoes are one of the strongest prepared foods available, healing 22 HP in a single bite, but they require several steps: cooking a potato, preparing or buying butter, adding the butter to the potato, cooking tuna and chopping it in a bowl, cooking sweetcorn and adding it to the same bowl, and combining the final ingredients.

Dairy products such as cream, butter and cheese are made using a dairy churn. They are often used as recipe ingredients than as direct healing foods.

### Stews and Curry

Stews are made using bowls, water, cooked meat and other ingredients.

Common stew-like foods include:

- Stew

- Curry

- Spicy Stew

Spicy Stews will eventually be unlocked through the Evil Dave section of Recipe for Disaster. They are mainly useful for temporary stat boosts rather than ordinary healing.

### Wine and Brewing

Jug of Wine is made by using grapes on a jug of water and waiting for it to ferment. It gives fast Cooking experience and can be made at a bank, but it lowers Attack when drunk.

Wine can still be useful outside melee combat, especially when healing after failed Thieving attempts or when using Ranged or Magic in a situation where the Attack penalty does not matter.

Players can also brew ales and cider. Brewing is slower and less commonly used for training, but some drinks provide useful temporary skill boosts. Chef's Delight is especially relevant because it can temporarily boost Cooking.

### Gnome Cuisine

Gnome Cuisine is a Cooking activity based in the Grand Tree. It includes gnome cocktails, crunchies, battas and bowls.

Examples include:

- Fruit Blast

- Toad Crunchies

- Worm Crunchies

- Toad Batta

- Cheese and Tomato Batta

- Chocolate Bomb

Gnome foods are more specialized than fish and are usually made for quests, Gnome Restaurant deliveries, or novelty rather than routine training.

### Other Specialty Foods

Some foods are tied to specific quests, regions or activities.

Notable examples include:

- Ugthanki Kebab

- Chompy Bird Meat

- Jubbly Bird Meat

- Nettle Tea

These foods are worth knowing about, but most players do not use them as primary Cooking training methods.

## Useful Locations

### Lumbridge Castle

The Lumbridge Castle range is one of the best early Cooking locations after Cook's Assistant. It reduces burn chance for many low-level foods and is close to the bank upstairs.

The Culinaromancer's Chest in the Lumbridge Castle basement becomes available during Recipe for Disaster. It acts as a bank and sells many useful Cooking supplies such as grapes, butter, bowls, apples, etc. Unlike most other shops, it restocks daily.

### Rogue's Den

The Rogue's Den under the Burthorpe tavern contains an everlasting fire very close to a bank. It is one of the fastest places to cook food that can be cooked on a fire.

This is especially useful for bulk fish cooking when burn rates are acceptable, particularly with Cooking Gauntlets.

### Catherby

Catherby is a popular Fishing and Cooking area because fishing spots, a range and a bank are all nearby.

It is especially useful for players gathering their own fish, including Lobsters, Swordfish, Tuna, and Sharks. The Fishing Guild to the southwest, north of Ardougne, is worth evaluating as well.

### Cooking Guild

The Cooking Guild is located west of Varrock and requires 32 Cooking and a Chef's Hat to enter.

The guild contains Cooking supplies, ranges, a sink, a churn and a mill. It is useful for gathering ingredients and making more complex foods. Bank access requires the appropriate Varrock achievement reward, so it is not always the fastest place to train.

### Sinclair Mansion

Sinclair Mansion provides an unlimited flour source. Bring empty pots and fill them there instead of milling grain manually.

## Training Tips

- Use the Lumbridge Castle range for early Cooking after completing Cook's Assistant.

- Cook fish while training Fishing if you want passive Cooking experience without buying supplies.

- Banked raw fish are usually faster than gathering everything yourself.

- Buy raw fish if your goal is the fastest Cooking experience.

- Use Cooking Gauntlets for higher-level fish as soon as they are available.

- Use the Rogue's Den fire for fast banked cooking when cooking food that works on a fire.

- Use wines for very fast Cooking experience, but remember that they are not ideal melee combat food.

- Avoid expensive high-level foods if your burn rate is still high.

- Keep some low-cost fish for feeding kittens if you are raising cats for death runes.

- Use prepared foods like Pineapple Pizzas, Tuna Potatoes and Summer Pies when their special properties are more useful than simple one-click healing.

## Summary

Cooking is one of the fastest and most practical skills to train. Fish provide the simplest path from early levels to high-level combat food, while wines offer very fast experience for players who are bankstanding.

Beyond fish, Cooking includes many useful food categories: pies, pizzas, potatoes, cakes, stews, dairy, wine, gnome cuisine and quest-related specialty foods. These foods are often slower to make than fish, but they provide useful healing patterns, stat boosts or quest utility.

For most players, the best long-term approach is to train mainly with fish or wine, unlock Cooking Gauntlets, use efficient locations such as Lumbridge or the Rogue's Den, and prepare specialty foods only when their extra effects are useful.
