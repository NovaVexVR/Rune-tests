# Agility

Agility is a skill that provides access to various shortcuts and helps determine the rate at which your run energy is depleted and restored. Training Agility allows you to navigate the world faster and is therefore highly recommended.

## Training

 Levels | Course | Notes | 

 1-30 | Gnome Agility Course | Safest early training option, obstacles cannot be failed. See notes below on travel tips to reach this course. | 

 30-52 | Agility Pyramid | At lower levels, the plank obstacle should be repeated, as it provides great experience rates. Higher Hitpoints and food are recommended due to obstacle damage. Players should also bring 4-6 waterskins. | 

 52-99 | Wilderness Agility Course | Best long-term training option. Be aware that this course is located in the Wilderness, so only bring items you are willing to risk. | 

Players also have the option to skip agility levels, especially early ones, with the quests listed below.

Players training Agility early should bring food, especially at lower Agility and Hitpoints levels, as failed obstacles can deal damage.

## Alternative Methods

 Levels | Course | Notes | 1x/hr | 

 15-30/52 | Edgeville Dungeon Monkey Bars | Easily accessed and can be clicked with minimal mouse movement. Bring food due to nearby monsters and poison risk. | 12,000 | 

 1-52 | Nature Grotto Bridge | Requires access to Morytania and Nature Spirit started. Minimal intensity, but bring food for failed attempts, especially at lower levels. | 18,000 | 

 1-40 | Brimhaven Agility Arena | Semi-implemented. Currently has several technical issues and is not recommended for training, even with higher potential experience rate, as players may repeatedly fail obstacles and take excessive damage, face clipping and out-of-bounds issues, etc. | | 

Other agility courses are currently not implemented.

## Travel Tips

The Edgeville Wilderness lever currently remains available and can be used as a convenient route to Ardougne and Kandarin. From there, players can travel to the Tree Gnome Stronghold to begin Agility training.

Other useful routes for low level players include:

- Games necklace teleport to the Barbarian Outpost, then travel west through Ardougne to the Tree Gnome Stronghold.

- Grand Exchange spirit tree after completing Tree Gnome Village. Completion of the quest is required to teleport to the Tree Gnome Stronghold spirit tree, but completion of The Grand Tree is required to teleport from them.

- Charter ships or the (Port Sarim)→Karamja→Brimhaven route to Ardougne.

- Fairy Rings after the completion of Lost City, notably the Edgeville ring easily accessible from Lumbridge via canoe.

- Ardougne teleport once unlocked.

## Quests

The following quests have an Agility level requirement:

 Quest | Level | 

 The Lost Tribe | 17 | 

 Roving Elves | 56 | 

The following quests award Agility experience:

 Quest | x1 | x2.5 | x5 | 

 The Tourist Trap | 4,650 | 11,625 | 23,250 | 

 Recruitment Drive | 1,000 | 2,500 | 5,000 | 

- The experience rewards for The Tourist Trap may be assigned to one of four skills: Agility, Fletching, Smithing, or Thieving. This reward choice is choosable twice.

Quest rewards can significantly expedite early Agility training:

- At 1× XP, Recruitment Drive alone takes Agility from level 1 to level 9.

- At 1× XP, completing Recruitment Drive and assigning both Tourist Trap rewards to Agility takes Agility from level 1 to level 26.

- At 2.5× XP, completing Recruitment Drive and assigning both Tourist Trap rewards to Agility takes Agility from level 1 to level 35.

- At 5× XP, completing Recruitment Drive and assigning both Tourist Trap rewards to Agility takes Agility from level 1 to level 42.
