# Thieving

Thieving allows players to steal from NPCs, stalls, chests, and various other objects throughout Gielinor. As players gain higher Thieving levels, they unlock new methods of making money and gaining experience, including pickpocketing higher tier NPCs, stealing from market stalls, and completing activities such as Pyramid Plunder.

## Getting Started

Most players begin training by pickpocketing low-level NPCs (Man/Woman in Lumbridge) before progressing to stalls and more rewarding targets.

Failure while pickpocketing may result in being stunned and taking damage. Using prayers that boost healing or having food available can improve training efficiency.

## Chests

Several locked chests around Gielinor can be looted for experience and rewards. Many chests, such as the Ourg bone coffins under Jiggig, are not implemented.

## Rogues' Den

The Rogues' Den minigame located beneath Burthorpe Inn is not implemented. However, the lobby under the bar can be entered with 50 Thieving. A bank and the permanent fire for cooking EXP is present, and is fairly easy to access using the Games Necklace. 

Wall Safes within the Rogue's Den do not give great thieving experience, but they are very low effort and only require clicking in place while equipped with a stethoscope. It can be useful for e.g. mobile players or low-engagement training. It rewards you with uncut Rubies, Sapphires, Emeralds, and the occasional Diamond, which give decent money and resources for crafting, jewellery, enchanting, and high-level alchemy.

## Sorceress's Garden

The Sorceress's Garden is a stealth/thieving Minigame after quest Prince Ali Rescue. Talk to Osman just north of the Al-Kharid palace, then head to the southeast of the city to enter the minigame by talking to the Sorceress's apprentice. 

This stealth minigame grants Thieving Experience directly on clearing any chamber. Players sneak past elementals to steal Sq'irk Fruit, which can be turned into Sq'irkjuice for larger experience rewards after looting the same chamber multiple times. Useful for getting to 21 or 31 Thieving before Pyramid Plunder is available.

## Pyramid Plunder

The quest Icthlarin's Little Helper is not implemented, but Pyramid Plunder and some of the facilities in Sophanem are available. Clearing the requirements for the quest will allow you to use the underground bank in Sophanem. A minimum Thieving level of 21 is required, with a new chamber available every 10 levels that gives drastically boosted experience.

The first time you tackle the pyramid, you must talk to the explorer to its south. Afterwards, each time you wish to enter, you must check one of the 4 entrances until you find the one that has a mummy to begin the minigame and rewards you with Thieving experience.

Players search urns, chests and sarcophagi the Jalsavrah pyramid for treasure, which can be sold to Simon Templeton at the Agility Pyramid just north-east from Sophanem (cannot be noted). Pyramid Plunder is one of the fastest methods of training Thieving at higher levels, with a chance of rewarding players with the Pharaoh's Sceptre for high sale value, or useful teleports to various locations within the Kharidian Desert. It also offers decent money-making, on the scale of 150k-250k/hour.

Pyramid Plunder is by far the best method to level up Thieving past level 91, and even past level 81, but is effective and scales at all levels.

## Blackjacking

Blackjacking is one of the fastest methods of training Thieving in the mid-levels, but is very active.

Players lure Pollnivneach bandits into nearby buildings, knock them unconscious using a blackjack, then repeatedly pickpocket them before they recover. This method requires constant attention but provides excellent experience rates from level 45 onward.

## Training

- Pickpocket Men/Women until level 5.

- Train on Farmers and H.A.M. Members (great for Easy Clue Scrolls) early. The Tea Stall in Varrock is also useful at low levels for a minor attack-boosting and healing item.

- Master Farmers provide valuable Farming seeds and solid experience.

- Stalls can give various useful items, with a wide variety of options to tackle at various levels.

- Ardougne Knights are a great experience method with decent rewards once unlocked.

- Food/wines for healing, and equipment like the Gloves of Silence (from Hunter), reduce downtime while training due to failed pickpockets.

- Rogue's Den training is slow, but gives some decent rewards and consistent/useful loot.

- Pyramid Plunder becomes one of the fastest training methods at higher levels.
