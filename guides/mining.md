# Mining

Mining is a skill that allows the extraction of ores from rocks. A higher Mining level provides a higher chance of success when swinging at rocks.

## Pickaxes

All pickaxes, except the Inferno adze, can be bought at Nurmof's Pickaxe Shop in the Dwarven Mines. Pickaxe-Is-Mine in Keldagrim also stocks Bronze, Steel, Mithril, Adamant, and Rune pickaxes for the same price. The Inferno adze is rewarded from the All Fired Up minigame.

They increase your mining odds by multiplying your own mining power.
The mining cape also increases your mining power.

## Rocks

You can mine many rocks. To know what it has, you can Prospect it first. You can also check the visuals, even though some are similar (like tin and silver for me).

Since the ores never swap places, you will know your usual spots by heart in no time.

It's written “Exp per ore” because extra ores also give exp. You can get an extra with shooting star's sprite buff or Varock armor.

## Training tips

Firstly, always use the best pickaxe available for your mining level. It's okay to skip the early upgrades, but as soon as you can use a Rune Pickaxe, please do. It increases your odds to mine when the mine check tick comes (4th game tick).

I heard Shooting stars are always a great option to train. But I didn't tested nor I have formulas to check it. Still, probably true.

### Math

The odds to get an ore are as follows:

- First you get the “player” side of the odds.

- Get your mining level minus the req. level for the ore.

- Increase by the pickaxe power multiplier, plus the skill cape if you have it.

- A random number between 0 and that number is generated.

- Then you get the “rock” side of the odds.

- A random number between 0 and the hardness of that rock is generated.

- If the player number is higher, it wins against the rock and you'll get its ore. 

## Shooting Stars

Shooting Stars crash periodically around 2009scape. There are 9 tiers, 1-9, with a Mining level of the tier x 10 is required to mine it. See Shooting Stars.
