# Herblore

Herblore is the skill of cleaning herbs and combining them with secondary ingredients to create potions. Potions provide a wide variety of useful effects including stat boosts, poison cures, prayer restoration, and combat enhancements. Training Herblore can be expensive, but it is extremely useful in almost any circumstance.

## Getting Started

Before Herblore can be trained, players must complete the quest Druidic Ritual, which grants access to the skill.

Most potions are created in three steps:

- Clean a grimy herb.

- Use the clean herb on a vial of water to create an unfinished potion.

- Add the required secondary ingredient to complete the potion.

Higher Herblore levels unlock stronger potions and more profitable recipes. Some potions, both for quests and outside of them, require a multistep brewing process. Vials can be created via glassblowing, or most easily obtained by buying them from NPC shops.

## Cleaning Herbs

Cleaning grimy herbs grants a small amount of Herblore experience and is required before they can be used to make potions.

Clean Marrentill is also used in Player Owned Home altars with incense burners to further increase EXP rewards.

## Unfinished Potions

Using a clean herb on a vial of water creates an unfinished potion, which does not grant any experience. No additional ingredients are required until the final step.

## Potions

Herblore also includes Barbarian Herblore, allowing players to create potion mixes that heal Hitpoints in addition to providing their normal effects. Potion mixes are currently not supported in 2009Scape.

Prayer Potions are bread-and-butter for many types of bosses, dungeons, and slayer tasks. At higher levels, Herblore can also be used to create Summoning potions, which restore Summoning points and are useful when training or using familiars.

## Training

- Complete Druidic Ritual immediately to unlock the skill.

- Clean every herb you obtain.

- Save valuable herbs such as Ranarr for higher levels. Prayer potions and Super Restores are generally worth making rather than selling the herbs.

- Many enemies drop herbs, but many of the herbs you'll obtain will be through Farming. Seeds can be obtained from enemies, Pickpocketing Master Farmers, or bought from the Vinesweeper shop.
