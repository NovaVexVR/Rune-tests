# Crafting

## Getting Started

Crafting allows players to create jewellery, leather/dragonhide armour, glass items, pottery, and battlestaves. Materials can be gathered through Mining, Woodcutting, Hunter, and general combat.

At low levels, Crafting can be trained cheaply by making leather armour, glass items, or jewellery. Dragonhide bodies and elemental Battlestaves provide both experience and solid profit at higher levels.

A needle, thread, chisel, moulds, glassblowing pipe or other tools are required depending on the item being crafted.

## Tips

- Flax can be picked in Seers' Village or Tree Gnome Stronghold and spun into bow strings, providing small amounts of Crafting experience. This synergizes with training fletching and moneymaking with high level alchemy.

- Al Kharid is the best location for early leather and hard leather crafting, with Ellis the Tanner located a short distance from the bank, and cowhides available east of Lumbridge.

- Seaweed can be collected in various places like Entrana, Karamja, Piscatoris, and Relleka, cooked to create soda ash, and combined with buckets of sand to make molten glass. Going through the dialogue with the general store NPC in Catherby offers 80 Seaweed per day at the price of 2 gold each (unnoted). Stock up on molten glass to use for unpowered orbs. These are commonly empowered at the air obelisk and crafted into air orbs/battlestaves for high-level alchemy.

- Yanille and Zanaris offer a way to stock up on buckets of sand, which that are fast to collect and near a bank. Zanaris and Entrana also offer a convenient furnace if not using Superglass Make, or crafting in either Edgeville or Shilo Village for the banks closest to a furnace.

- Uncut Ruby, Sapphire, and Emeralds for chiseling exp and crafting gold jewellry can be obtained easily from the floor safes in the Rogue's Den in Burthrope with level 50+ thieving and a stethoscope, right next to bank. Opals, Jades, and Topazes are obtained from the Shilo Village gem rocks.
