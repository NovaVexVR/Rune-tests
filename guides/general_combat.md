# 
General Combat Guide

# Basics

## Combat Level

The combat level is a single numerical indicator estimating a characters overall strength. The higher it is, the stronger the enemy is. Hovering over a player or NPC shows their combat level. The color tells you how big the level difference is between you and your target. Green means that the target is weaker than you, yellow that you are roughly equal, and red that the target is stronger. This indicator generally often does not reflect reality in terms of combat performance.

Combat level is calculated slightly differently for NPCs and players. Both share combat skills in common: Attack, Strength, Defense, Magic, Ranged, Prayer and Summoning. A player's combat level is calculated by the above skills with a maximum value of 138. 

For NPCs, factors beyond their raw skill levels contribute to their combat effectiveness. NPCs may possess armor, weapons, or various special attacks, all of which are important when evaluating their strength in combat. For example, a Black Dragon has natural armor in the form of scales, can breathe fire with a higher “max hit” or potential max damage value, and uses its claws and teeth as natural weapons for melee damage. These traits are all reflected in its overall combat level.

## Combat areas

There are two types of combat areas in 2009Scape: single combat and multi-combat.

Single combat is an exclusive one-on-one combat mode. Only one opponent can attack you at a time, whether you are fighting another player or an NPC. Likewise, you can only attack one target at a time. This is the most common type of combat area.

Multi-combat areas allow multiple players and NPCs to engage the same target simultaneously. This means fights such as two-versus-one, two-versus-two, or larger battles are possible. You can identify a multi-combat area by the crossed swords icon displayed in the lower-right corner of the game screen.

Be cautious when entering multi-combat areas. They can be far more dangerous than single combat areas, as multiple enemies may attack you at once. Without protection, weak-to-mid tier enemies will potentially be able to chip away at your HP.

## Hitpoints

Hitpoints represent your character's health. There are two ways to monitor them during gameplay.

The first is the health bar that appears above your character whenever you take damage. The green portion shows your remaining hitpoints, while the red portion indicates the amount of health you have lost. Similar health bars are displayed above your opponents during combat.

The second is the hitpoints counter located in the upper-right corner of the game screen, next to the minimap. It is displayed as a red heart with your current hitpoints shown as a number.

Always keep an eye on your hitpoints, especially during difficult fights. If they get too low, eat food to restore them. Different foods restore different amounts of hitpoints. See the Cooking article for a list of common foods.

Whenever you take damage, a hitsplat appears above your character. Hitsplats display the amount of damage dealt and, in some cases, the type of damage you received.

If your hitpoints become critically low, it is often best to retreat. Most NPCs will only pursue you for a short distance before returning to their original location. However, escape is not always possible. If your hitpoints reach zero, your character will die.

## Damage Types

Damage in 2009Scape is divided into several types. Understanding how each type works will help you choose the most effective equipment and combat style.

### Combat damage

The three main combat styles deal different types of damage. Your Defense level reduces an opponent's chance to hit you, but it does not reduce the damage of a successful hit.

#### Melee

Melee damage is identified by the sword icon on hitsplats.

Melee attacks are divided into three damage types:

- Stab

- Slash

- Crush

Every NPC and piece of armor has different defensive bonuses against these damage types. Choosing a weapon that targets an opponent's weakest defense will improve your chance to hit.

General examples:

- Stab is effective against dragons and opponents wearing chainmail.

- Slash performs well against lightly armored targets.

- Crush is effective against plate armor and other heavily armored opponents.

#### Ranged

Ranged damage is identified by the bow icon on hitsplats.

This damage type includes all non-magical projectiles, including:

- Arrows

- Bolts

- Darts

- Throwing knives

- Javelins

- Throwing axes

Ranged attacks are generally most effective against opponents with low Ranged defense.

#### Magic

Magic damage is identified by the wizard hat icon on hitsplats.

Unlike melee, the elemental type of a combat spell has little effect on accuracy or damage. Instead, the target's Magic defense is the primary factor.

Magic is especially effective against heavily armored opponents, while many demons have particularly low Magic defense.

Magic defense is calculated differently from melee and ranged defense. It is based on:

- 70% of your Magic level

- 30% of your Defense level

- Your equipment's Magic defense bonus

Because of this, training Magic also improves your resistance to magical attacks, even if you primarily use melee or ranged.

### Special damage types

#### Dragonfire

Dragonfire is the signature attack of adult dragons and can deal extremely high damage without proper protection.

Protection is available through:

- Anti-dragon shield

- Protect from Magic

- Antifire potion

- Super antifire potion

The following combinations completely negate standard dragonfire damage:

- Anti-dragon shield + Antifire potion

- Anti-dragon shield + Protect from Magic

- Antifire potion + Protect from Magic

Dragonfire is not classified as Magic damage, although equipment with high Magic defense also helps reduce its effectiveness.

Most dragons only use dragonfire against targets standing directly in front of them. Metal dragons can breathe dragonfire from a distance, while young dragons cannot use dragonfire at all.

#### Poison

Poison is a damage-over-time effect.

You can become poisoned by:

- Poisonous NPC attacks

- Poisoned weapons

- Certain traps

- Smoke spells from the Ancient Magicks spellbook

After becoming poisoned, you will receive a chat message and periodically take damage. The damage gradually decreases over time until the poison wears off naturally.

Poison can be cured with an Antipoison potion or stronger antidotes. These also provide temporary immunity to poison, with stronger potions granting longer protection.

Certain spells from the Lunar Spellbook can cure poison, but they do not provide temporary immunity.

Some NPCs, particularly poisonous creatures, are immune to poison.

# Fighting Styles

There are three primary combat styles in 2009Scape:

- Melee

- Ranged

- Magic

Each style has strengths and weaknesses against the others, forming the combat triangle.

 Combat Style | Strong Against | Weak Against | 

 Melee | Ranged | Magic | 

 Ranged | Magic | Melee | 

 Magic | Melee | Ranged | 

This relationship is largely determined by equipment. Different armor types provide different defensive bonuses, making them naturally stronger against certain attack styles and weaker against others. However, combat outcomes also depend on factors such as levels, equipment, prayers, potions, and player skill, so the combat triangle should be treated as a general guideline rather than a strict rule.

The same principles apply when fighting both players and NPCs.

## Melee

Melee focuses on close-range combat using weapons such as swords, scimitars, maces, and warhammers.

Melee armor generally provides:

- High melee defense

- High ranged defense

- Low Magic defense

This makes melee fighters durable against physical attacks, but vulnerable to Magic.

Melee weapons require no ammunition, making them inexpensive to use over long periods. Because melee fighters remain in close combat, they generally consume more food than ranged attackers.

Melee can also take advantage of flinching. By attacking an NPC once, stepping out of its attack range until it becomes passive, then attacking again, players can defeat some strong melee opponents while taking little or no damage. 2-ranged melee weapons like Halberds can also take limited advantage of safespots. Safespotting involves attacking an enemy over impassable terrain or geometry to safely deal damage without retaliation, or while forcing the enemy into one specific attack style that can be negated by gear, potions, or Prayer buffs.

Melee is one of the most versatile combat styles and performs well against a wide variety of NPCs.

## Ranged

Ranged attacks opponents from a distance using bows, crossbows, thrown weapons, and other projectiles.

Ranged armor generally provides:

- High Magic defense

- Moderate ranged defense

- Low melee defense

This makes ranged users effective against Magic users while leaving them more vulnerable to melee attacks.

Ranged is the combat style most commonly used for safespotting. Obstacles such as rocks, trees, fences, or other scenery can prevent many NPCs from reaching you while you continue attacking from a distance.

The primary ongoing cost of Ranged combat is ammunition.

## Magic

Magic uses combat spells powered by runes to attack enemies from a distance.

Magic robes generally provide:

- High Magic attack bonuses

- Moderate Magic defense

- Low melee and ranged defense

Magic excels against heavily armored opponents because most melee armor provides poor Magic defense. Many spells also offer utility effects beyond damage, such as binding or freezing targets.

Like Ranged, Magic can also make extensive use of safespots to attack NPCs without taking damage. Binding spells can further prevent targets from reaching you, making Magic especially effective against melee opponents.

Combat Magic is typically the most expensive combat style due to rune costs, but it offers excellent versatility through its wide range of offensive and utility spells.

# Summoning

Summoning itself is not a fighting style, but that does not mean it should be treated lightly. It is an interesting and powerful supplement to the other styles, and the variety of summons ensures everyone can find something suitable for their needs in both skilling and combat.

## Hybrid fighting style

Having several fighting styles at once, especially in PvP combat, is certainly useful. you can thanks surprise the opponent. For this tactic to be effective, you need to take an extra set of armor and weapons, and you can tailor your armor to be ready to use with your chosen styles simultaneously. Both concepts have their drawbacks, inventory for two styles at once takes up space in backpack, which could be occupied, for example, by food. Equipment adapted to two types attacks, in turn, often has a potentially lower defense or negatively affects the accuracy of attacks. It's worth it test both options and choose the best one.

The following combinations are most often used:

- Melee + Ranged

- Melee + Magic

# Weapon Types

A good weapon in hand is essential, and choosing the most appropriate one when preparing for a fight is a real art. An art worth mastering, and the fight will become much easier. Below you will find general characteristics main types of weapons will help you choose the right one.

## Melee weapons

Daggers

Small and very fast weapons mainly used for stabbing, but are also suitable for cuts. However, their strength is weak and they are only suitable for fighting the weaker, unarmored ones enemies. For players, members can become useful if they become poisoned. One-handed weapon.

Swords

As fast as daggers, and stronger. They are used to inflict stab wounds and cut. One-handed weapon.

Longswords

Slightly slower than daggers and short swords, but much stronger than them, swords long attacks deal accurate piercing and slashing damage. One-handed weapon.

Scimitars

As fast as daggers, much stronger than them, but weaker than them long swords. They deal effective slash damage, a weak piercing weapon. Very popular due to a good compromise between strength and speed. One-handed weapon.

Maces

They have the speed of longswords. Good crush damage weapon however, they can also inflict stab wounds. They have an interesting property of increasing the bonus to prayer. One-handed weapon. 

Hatchets

Their main use is chopping wood and should not be used in combat. If necessary, they inflict slash and crush wounds. They have the same speed as longswords. They don't impress with strength. One-handed weapon.

Pickaxes

Like axes, they are not very suitable for combat, serving mainly miners. If there is nothing better at hand, they have the speed of longswords, they inflict crushing and piercing wounds. One-handed weapon.

Battlexes

Slightly slower than longswords, but much stronger than them. It causes wounds cut and crushed. One-handed weapon.

Warhammers

A weapon similar to war axes, similarly fast and strong, but only inflicts crush injuries. One-handed weapon

Two-handed swords

Incredibly slow and equally incredibly powerful, two-handed swords crush and slash everything on its way. They are also very accurate weapons.

Spears

A flexible weapon capable of dealing crushing, piercing and slashing damage. However, this comes at the cost of strength and speed. Spears have the speed of longswords and can be poisoned. Two-handed weapon.

Claws

Claws are as fast as daggers, they are better at inflicting slashing wounds, but they cannot be poisoned. Two-handed weapon.

Halberds

As slow as two-handed swords and not as powerful as them, they are still capable of dealing many injuries. They are great for stabbing, a bit less for slashing. the most interesting of them feature is that they can attack opponents not directly in front of you, also those hiding behind small obstacles. Two-handed weapon.

## Ranged weapons

Longbows

Longbows are quite slow weapons, but they are more accurate and stronger than shortbows. He also has greater range. Bows shoot arrows that can be poisoned. Two-handed weapon.

Shortbows

A fast weapon to fight low defense opponents. They don't impress with their accuracy and attack power. Two-handed weapon.

Crossbows

Crossbows use bolts as ammo, they shoot as fast as longbows, they are quite accurate and deal very decent damage. Bolts can be poisoned or equipped with gemstone overlays that, when enchanted, produce magical effects. There are crossbows one-handed weapon.

Darts

Darts are probably one of the fastest weapons, but they pay for it with a small force. However, against unarmored opponents, it is a very dangerous weapon. They can be poisoned. One-handed weapon.

Knives

As fast as darts, but slightly stronger and with a slightly longer range. Same like darts can be poisoned. One-handed weapon.

## Magic weapons

Staves, Battlestaves, Mystic Battlestaves

Mages use magic staffs that give them attack bonuses and magical defense. They are not very suitable as Melee weapons, as they have the speed of long swords and deal crush damage. Battlestaves and Mystic Battlestaves have improved melee and magic stats. These are one-handed weapons, so they can be used with the anti-dragon shield.

Standard staves and battlestaves can autocast magic from the Standard spellbook. The God staves from the Mage Arena, Iban's Staff, and the Slayer Staff are required for casting certain spells. 

The Ancient Staff obtainable after Desert Treasure can autocast from both the Ancient and Standard spellbooks. 

# Armour Types

Solid armor is as good as a sharp sword. Reduces the chance of hitting the opponent's attacks, which you don't have to constantly worry about your health and other food. However, keep in mind your fighting style, which you chose. Certain types of armor interfere with the use of certain combat techniques, affecting badly accuracy of attacks. Armor descriptions can be found below.

## Warrior armors

Med helmets

These helmets offer rather low protection, but their advantage is that they don't have much negative modifiers to Ranged and Magic attacks. They are also a very cheap item equipment.

Full Helmets

This solid head protection is a choice for warriors, while a bad one for archers and mages who are unlikely to be able to attack accurately with this piece of iron on head. Like all types of metal armor, it makes the owner more vulnerable to magic.

Chainmail

Chain mail is armor that protects well against slashing and crushing damage, her the weakness is puncture damage. Can also be used by claim archers better defense, because there are no negative bonuses to attack from a distance. Just like armor plate, not suitable for mages.

Platebody, Platelegs

This heavy armor offers excellent melee protection, well protected against bullets. Completely unsuitable for mages and archers, the owner is also vulnerable it's for magic attacks. His weakness is crush damage. It often doesn't belong either cheap.

Barrows Armour

These powerful armor sets are one of the best forms of protection for warriors. The defensive bonuses of this armor are definitely impressive, each set more so has a special effect when used in its entirety. They require periodic repairs that cost a bit of money, but are definitely worth it. Verac kit has a slightly weaker defense, but offers a considerable bonus to Prayer.

Square Shields

These shields offer quite good defense, slightly weaker than kiteshields. Mages and archers can use them for extra protection at the cost of a small drop accuracy of attacks. Their weakness is crush attacks. Kiteshields Kiteshields have better bonuses than square shields, although they are heavier. They are little more vulnerable to stab attacks. The most common type of shield.

Anti-dragon Shield

It doesn't offer much defense, but in most cases it's essential against members of the dragon family. Mages and archers may like this shield for its no negative modifiers. Using Draconic Visage you can significantly shield this shield improve.

Dragonfire Shield

It's an upgraded anti-dragon shield, it absorbs dragon's breath every time becoming stronger. It becomes very heavy then, but it has some defensive bonuses higher among the shields. In addition, it can shoot dragon fire.

Rockshell Armour

This armor made from stone crab shells is often worn by Fremennik warriors. Offers almost the same defensive bonuses as rune armor.

Granite Armour

Armor for definitely the toughest. It's terribly heavy, but with bonuses it outperforms rune armor and also has high durability against z attacks distance.

Bandos Armour

Armor dedicated to the god of war offers excellent defensive stats, by the way increasing the strength of its bearer. It also has an interesting appearance.

Defenders

While not a 'traditional' armor, Defenders act as an offhand alternative to shields for physical builds. Defenders (up to Rune) can be obtained from the Warriors Guild in Burthrope. They must be farmed individually and cannot be traded, and offer defense and strength bonuses alongside significant attack bonuses.
