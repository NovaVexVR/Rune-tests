# Construction

Construction allows players to build and customize a Player-Owned House (POH). Houses provide useful utilities including teleports, altars, workshops, storage, and various skilling conveniences. While Construction is one of the most expensive skills to train, a well-developed house offers long-term benefits throughout the game.

## Getting Started

To purchase a house, speak to any Estate Agent and pay 1,000 coins.

Your house can be moved between several locations by speaking to an Estate Agent again, with different prices and construction skill level requirements depending on location. House portals are located throughout Gielinor. You must enter your house via it's associated portal or by using the Home Teleport spell.

## Servants

Servants can be hired from the Servants' Guild in East Ardougne. They can bring planks from the bank, fetch building materials, and significantly reduce downtime while training.

Higher-tier servants are faster but charge higher wages.

## Training Tips

- For low level players, two premade planks can be obtained for free and constantly respawn on Entrana, in the northwest near the Law Altar.

- Build and immediately remove the whatever furniture is available for the fastest experience. Chairs, using regular planks and nails, are common early options.

- Use Oak Planks, especially with items that don't need nails such as the Repair Bench, Crafting Table, Dining Tables, and eventually Oak Larders. Experience is based on the 'expected' materials used, so everything that requires 2 Oak Planks and no other materials will always gives 120 EXP at 1x.

- Hire the best servant you can afford to reduce banking time. Butler banking becomes increasingly valuable at higher Construction levels.

- A mounted Glory, Portal Chamber and Chapel are among the most useful long-term upgrades.

- Keep a saw, hammer and sufficient planks in your inventory while training. You can build tool shelves containing saw and hammer items into your home to avoid having to prep and carry them around.

- Teak Planks allow for efficient training, and Mahogany Planks enable maximum experience at a high price.

## Money Saving

Construction is a fast but expensive skill to train. You can reduce costs by doing the following:

- Cut and process your own logs into planks.

- Purchase planks during periods of lower Grand Exchange prices.

- Use servants efficiently to maximize experience per hour.

- Plan furniture upgrades to avoid unnecessary rebuilding.

Non-Ironman players can leverage facilities from a friends POH if they are online and in their house, most commonly their Gilded Altar.
