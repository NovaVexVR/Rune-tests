# Ranged Training Guide

Ranged excels at attacking from a distance while avoiding damage through safespots, damaging susceptible enemies, and offering solid mixed defenses. It is commonly trained using bows, crossbows, darts, knives, the occasional other thrown weapons, and chinchompas. It can leverage area-of-effect attacks in certain cases. 

## General tips

- Use the Rapid attack style for the fastest experience.

- Complete Animal Magnetism as early as possible to obtain Ava's Attractor, which helps conserve ammunition while training. Upgrade it at level 50.

- Train on monsters with high Hitpoints and low Ranged defense whenever possible.

- Make use of safespots to avoid unnecessary damage.

- Train Ranged alongside Slayer whenever practical to gain experience in both skills.

The Dorgeshuun crossbow is one of the most cost-effective Ranged training weapons. It attacks one tick faster than most crossbows and uses inexpensive Bone bolts, making it an excellent choice for Rock Crabs, general training, and even low-level Slayer tasks. Crossbows in general are one-handed ranged weapons and pair well with the anti-dragon shield.

The Magic shortbow is the preferred weapon for general training thanks to its fast attack speed and strong damage output.

The Rune crossbow is generally reserved for tougher monsters and bosses where its superior accuracy and enchanted bolt effects outweigh its slower attack speed.

Knives and Darts both attack rapidly and can also be fantastic ammunition for training.

Longbows are worse for damage due to their slow attack speed, but there are niche situations where their additional range is beneficial, and they remain valuable fletching products due to being high value for high level alchemy.

Dragonhide armor provides strong Ranged accuracy and excellent Magic defense, making it ideal for both training and PvM. Black dragonhide is one of the most popular armor sets due to its combination of performance and affordability. 

Karil's equipment offers improved Magic defense, while Armadyl equipment provides the highest offensive bonuses.

### Recommended upgrades

As your budget increases, consider upgrading the following equipment:

- Ava's Attractor → Ava's Accumulator after completing Animal Magnetism. When not wearing metallic body armor, you will be able to automatically conserve ranged ammunition and generate low tier metal objects in your inventory (ores, weapons, etc.). The Attractor functionally multiplies your ammunition by 2.5, and the Accumulator multiplies it by 3.57

- Archer helm after completing The Fremennik Trials.

- Barrows gloves after progressing through Recipe for Disaster.

- Amulet of glory for improved combat bonuses.

- Ranging potion for difficult encounters.

For high-level PvM, a Rune crossbow paired with enchanted bolts is one of the strongest setups available. Common choices include:

- Ruby bolts (e) against bosses with high Hitpoints.

- Diamond bolts (e) against opponents with high Defense.

- Broad bolts for inexpensive Slayer training.

Note that enchanted bolts are currently bugged against non-Slayer enemies. A fix is scoped for the next update.

## Rock Crabs

Rock Crabs are one of the best early and mid-level training methods thanks to their high Hitpoints, low Defense, and minimal damage output. This allows for excellent AFK potential. As noted above, shortbows, the Dorgeshuun Crossbow + Bone Bolts, Darts, and Knives are all effective training tools.

Like many enemies, after approximately 10 minutes, Rock Crabs become passive. Move far enough away to reset their aggression before continuing to train.

## Chinchompas

Chinchompas are unique thrown weapons that explode on impact, damaging multiple nearby targets. Against tightly packed monsters, they provide some of the fastest Ranged experience available.

Popular training locations are:

- Skeleton monkeys after Monkey Madness (requirements) + Teleport to Ape Atoll.

- Ape Atoll Monkey Guards.

- Pest Control. Pest Control in general is a fantastic training area due to offering points that can be cashed in for boosted EXP rewards.

Because each explosion can damage several targets at once, chinchompas offer exceptional experience rates. They are expensive to use, making them best suited for players prioritizing fast experience over profit.

## Dwarf multicannon

The Dwarf multicannon is one of the fastest ways to train Ranged, albeit with some innate upfront and ongoing costs. The cannon attacks nearby enemies independently of your own attacks, allowing you to gain Ranged experience while fighting with any combat style.

The cannon is particularly effective against monsters that spawn in large numbers, in a multi-combat area, with low Defense, aggression, and that can be lured into the cannon's attack radius.

Popular cannon training locations include:

- Dagannoths in the Lighthouse after the requirements for Horror from the Deep.

- Rock Crabs.

- Slayer tasks with dense monster spawns.

Using a cannon during Slayer is recommended whenever practical. It significantly increases kill speed while providing passive Ranged experience alongside your primary combat style.

The main drawback is cost. The minimum cannon cost is 750k, and cannonballs are consumed quickly and often sell for 500-600 apiece on the GE, making cannon training considerably more expensive than conventional Ranged training. Because of this, many players reserve cannon use for difficult Slayer tasks or when maximizing experience rates.

## Slayer

Once your Ranged level is high, Slayer becomes an most efficient ways to continue training.

Many Slayer monsters can be safespotted, allowing you to conserve food while gaining experience in both skills, providing valuable drops, and preparing you for bossing and other high-level PvM encounters.
