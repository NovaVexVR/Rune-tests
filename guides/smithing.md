# Smithing

Smithing involves smelting ores into metal bars, and using a hammer to forge those bars into weapons, armour and ammunition. Smithing is closely tied to Mining, as most bars require ores obtained through mining or purchased from other players. Almost all players will find that their Mining level goes up faster than Smithing, even before considering the extra mining experience from Shooting Stars. Some enemies do drop ores or bars directly.

## Getting Started

Smithing consists of two stages:

- Smelting ores into metal bars using a furnace.

- Smithing bars into items at an anvil using a Hammer.

## Smelting

Different bars require different combinations of ores. Note that the Varrock Armour speeds up the rate of smelting at the Edgeville Furnace by occasionally smelting two bars at once, but does not actually increase the outputs in terms of total number of bars or experience.

 Level | Bar | Experience | Materials | 

 1 | Bronze | 6.2 | Copper Ore + Tin Ore | 

 15 | Iron | 12.5 | Iron Ore | 

 20 | Silver | 13.7 | Silver Ore | 

 30 | Steel | 17.5 | Iron Ore + 2 Coal | 

 40 | Gold | 22.5 / 56.2* | Gold Ore | 

 50 | Mithril | 30 | Mithril Ore + 4 Coal | 

 70 | Adamantite | 37.5 | Adamantite Ore + 6 Coal | 

 85 | Runite | 50 | Runite Ore + 8 Coal | 

*56.2 experience is awarded when wearing Goldsmith Gauntlets from the Family Crest quest.

The Blast Furnace Minigame is accessible at any level by reaching Keldagrim. Characters with Smithing below level 60 must pay 2500 gold to use the Blast Furnace for 10 minutes. The Blast Furnace is both directly faster for smelting (as in the case of Gold Bars) and consumes half as much coal (for Steel, Mithril, Adamant, and Rune bar smelting).

Silver and Gold bars are primarily smelted in a furnace along with moulds and cut gemstones for crafting. Gold is used in a variety of jewelry for later stringing and enchantment. Silver is primarily used for the Holy/Unholy Symbol, a few quest items like the Blessed Sickle, Silver crossbow bolts and Tiaras to be combined with excess talismans for Runecrafting EXP.

## Smithing

Most metal items are created by using bars on an anvil, with a few exceptions like gloves, boots, pickaxes, Defenders, etc. The experience gained depends entirely on the number of bars used to create the item.

 Bars Used | Example Items | Experience | 

 1 | Dagger, Hatchet, Mace, Nails | 12.5 × Bar XP | 

 2 | Scimitar, Full Helm, Square Shield | 25 × Bar XP | 

 3 | Platelegs, Plateskirt, Kiteshield | 37.5 × Bar XP | 

 5 | Platebody | 62.5 × Bar XP | 

## Cannonballs

At level 35 Smithing, players may smith Cannonballs using Steel Bars and a Cannonball Mould after completing Dwarf Cannon.

- 1 Steel Bar produces 4 Cannonballs.

- Grants 25.6 Smithing experience per Steel Bar.

- Very slow, but often profitable or useful for Ranged training.

## Training Tips

- Complete The Knight's Sword early as possible for a large Smithing experience reward.

- Always use a Ring of Forging when smelting Iron Bars, as it is inexpensive and guarantees successful smelts.

- Goldsmith Gauntlets dramatically increase experience when smelting Gold Bars, especially at the Blast Furnace.

- Smelt ores obtained through Mining whenever practical to reduce training costs.

- Smithing Platebodies provides the highest experience per bar and creates useful items for High Level Alchemy, while smaller items may be preferable for Fletching or Ranged training.

- A small quantity of nails is useful for early Construction and quests such as Dragon Slayer, but higher-level Construction generally relies exclusively on planks.

## Resources & Profit

Dwarven stout allows you to temporarily gain +1 Smithing and Mining levels, or +2 from the “mature” version. Depending on your specific level, this can enable you to smith useful items just above your current level for better profit, utility, or speed of smithing.

Smithing and Mining complement one another. Mining your own ores reduces training costs, while purchasing ore and/or bars generally offers much faster experience rates.

Smithing money makers include:

- Cannonballs and Steel Bars

- Adamant/Rune Dart Tips for selling and fletching/ranged training

- Platebodies for high level alchemy

- High-level Rune equipment
