# Melee Training

Melee combat is one of the fastest ways to progress your account in 2009scape. Training Attack, Strength, and Defence unlocks stronger equipment while preparing you for quests, Slayer, bossing, and PvP.

In general, prioritize Strength early on to increase your maximum hit, balanced with training Attack to unlock stronger weapons, before finishing with Defence. Whenever possible, use the best scimitar available, as they generally provide the fastest experience rates.

### Equipment Progression

Upgrading your weapon as soon as you meet its Attack requirement is one of the biggest improvements you can make to your melee training. In most situations, a better weapon will increase your experience rates far more than better armour.

New players can obtain a Steel Dagger very early by searching a building just across Lumbridge Bridge. It's a nice upgrade before you can afford or obtain your first scimitar.

### Useful Drops

Many popular low level melee training monsters provide resources that are useful for other skills.

- Chickens: Feathers for Fishing.

- Cows: Cowhides for Crafting.

- Hill Giants: Big Bones for Prayer and Limpwurt Roots.

- Moss Giants: Big Bones, herbs, seeds, and runes.

- Ogres: Big Bones, seeds

- Green Dragons: after beginning Dragon Slayer and gaining access to the anti-dragon shield, and/or buying antifire potions. Green Dragons can be accessed easily in the wilderness via the canoe system and give valuable drops, including guaranteed dragonhide and dragon bones.

- Slayer: Consistent profit and gradual experience to unlock higher tier monsters while training combat.

### Questing for Early Combat Levels

 Quest | Rewards | Comments | 

 Waterfall | Large Attack and Strength experience. | One of the best quests for new melee accounts, allowing you to skip many of the earliest combat levels. | 

 The Grand Tree | Attack experience. | A worthwhile follow-up that grants a substantial Attack boost while progressing the gnome questline. | 

 Fight Arena | Attack experience. | A quick source of Attack experience that pairs well with Waterfall and The Grand Tree. | 

 Vampire Slayer | Attack experience. | An easy early-game quest with a useful Attack reward for newer players. | 

Completing Waterfall Quest, The Grand Tree, and Fight Arena can raise your Attack high enough to equip stronger weapons with very little conventional training. After finishing these quests, players can move directly into training with Adamant or Rune equipment instead of spending time on the earliest combat levels. The Agility guide covers some method to reach these areas early.

However, most players do find the early-game progression through lower-tier weapons more enjoyable, as it is the time that the skills best keep up with and benefit from training non-combat skills like Smithing.
