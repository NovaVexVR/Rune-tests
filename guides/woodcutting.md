# Woodcutting

Woodcutting is one of the fan favorites of being the most relaxing skill to train in 2009scape. Fun fact that woodcutting is also Ceikry's favorite skill to train inside of 2009scape.

Woodcutting ties into several other skills. Logs can be burned for Firemaking, carved into bows and other equipment with Fletching, or turned into planks for Construction. Through Farming, players can also grow many varieties of trees in farming patches, giving them convenient locations to harvest logs while also training another skill.

In this guide we will be using both player made guides and just pure knowledge of how the game worked and what is inside of 2009scape. This page is a work in progress like the game itself. More content will be added to the game so more will be added to this Wiki in time.

### Daily Training

Before heading out to chop trees, it's worthvisiting the Woodcutting Tutor. Their daily jobs often provide some of the best experience for the time invested and are highly recommended for players of all levels.

Completing the Woodcutting Tutor's assignments is a great way to supplement your normal training while earning experience. Completing your daily job first is efficient use of your time and rewards you with GE-appropriate prices for your logs.

### Where to get an axe

For Ironmen, it should be noted that all axes besides dragon and the inferno adze can be obtained by killing Tree Spirits in the Enchanted Forest.

### Level 1-99 Training

There are several effective ways to train Woodcutting depending on whether your goal is the fastest experience, gathering resources for other skills, or making money. The following methods are recommended for most players.

 Levels | Tree | Accessible Locations | Comments | 

 1-20 | Normal Trees | Lumbridge, Varrock, Falador, almost every town. | Regular trees are plentiful throughout Gielinor, making the early levels quick and straightforward. | 

 20-35 | Oak Trees | Draynor Village, Varrock, Seers' Village. | Oak trees provide excellent early experience and are a solid source of logs for beginner Construction, as well as Firemaking and Fletching. | 

 30-75 | Willow Trees | Draynor Village, Barbarian Outpost, Seers' Village. | Willows are one of the most accessible training methods in the game, offering solid experience with minimal movement between trees. | 

 35-60+ | Teak Trees | Tai Bwo Wannai Hardwood Grove (freely accessible in 2009scape after the required quest completion with no trading stick cost). | Teaks provide the fastest traditional Woodcutting experience. They also benefit construction. | 

 60-75 | Maple Trees | Seers' Village, Sinclair Mansion | Maples are slower than Teaks but provide an excellent supply of logs for Firemaking and Fletching, making them a great choice to train multiple skills together. | 

 75-99 | Yew Trees | Edgeville, Seers' Village, Rimmington, Tree Gnome Stronghold. | Yews are slower experience but provide valuable logs and a much more AFK training method, making them a popular choice for long training sessions. |
