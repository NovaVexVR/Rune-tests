# Hunter

Hunter allows players to capture creatures using various items, such as traps, snares, butterfly nets and jars, and falconry. Successful captures reward Hunter experience and valuable resources including furs, feathers, herbs, chinchompas, and ingredients used in Herblore and Summoning.

## Getting Started

Most Hunter training consists of setting traps, waiting for creatures to enter them, then resetting the traps after a successful catch or failure.

As your Hunter level increases, you unlock additional trap placement types, counts, and creatures that are either more profitable or useful for training.

## Training Tips

- Currently, a glitch exists where if other traps are already placed, a Deadfall Trap cannot be placed afterwards. The Deadfall Trap must be placed first. Noose Wand Kebbit Burrows also may not work as expected and should likely be skipped.

- Kebbit spikes currently cannot be fletched into Kebbit Bolts by the player. They must instead be traded to the Huntsman in Yanille.

- The fastest way to gain early Hunter levels is from Vinesweeper. Ogleroots cost 10 coins each, every rabbit grants 30 Hunter experience (at 1x), and unused ogleroots are refunded. Alternatively, Bird Snares provide the fastest 'conventional' early progression.

- Many Hunter methods can be combined, such as placing Bird Snares or Deadfall Traps while catching butterflies. Note that unlike OSRS, butterflies do not provide stat boosts and can be released immediately to continue training.

- Visit a crop circle or Zanaris to reach Puro-Puro and obtain a Magic Butterfly Net. Trading filled impling jars for empty jars makes long hunting trips much easier. Although implings inside Puro-Puro give reduced Hunter experience, higher-tier implings provide valuable loot when released.

- Falconry offers excellent experience whenever a new kebbit becomes available. Spotted and Dashing Kebbit Fur can be exchanged for the Spotted Cape and Spottier Cape respectively, while Dark Kebbit Fur can be traded for Gloves of Silence to improve pickpocketing success.

- Red Chinchompas are among the best combinations of profit and experience, while also serving as powerful Ranged ammunition.

- Wear weight-reducing clothing and keep traps close together to minimize downtime.

## Summoning

Hunter supplies many secondary ingredients used in Summoning. Bird meat, kebbit fur, chinchompas, 'green salamanders' / swamp lizards, and other hunter rewards are used to create pouches.

## Herblore

Hunter supplies several ingredients used in Herblore:

- Kebbit Teeth (Hunter Potion)

- Bird Nests (Saradomin Brew)
