# Fletching

## Getting Started

Fletching allows players to create ranged equipment including bows, arrows, crossbows, bolts, and darts. After cutting down logs from a tree, you can use a knife to cut logs into bows or arrow shafts, attach bow strings to unstrung bows, and create arrows, bolts, and darts by combining their components with feathers, arrowheads, or dart tips.

Fletching can be trained passively by making darts or arrows while training other skills or during downtime between activities. It is also an excellent money-making skill by creating and High Level Alchemising Yew and Magic bows, which can also be done while performing other activities. A strung bow has approximately double the High Level Alchemy value of its unstrung counterpart.

A knife is required for most Fletching activities involving logs, while feathers, arrowheads, bow strings and other materials are needed to finish ammunition and weapons.

## Arrows

Arrows are created in three stages:

* Cut normal logs into arrow shafts using a knife.
* Attach feathers to create headless arrows.
* Attach arrowheads to create finished arrows.

## Tips

* Ten Bow strings can be purchased from Uglug near Jiggig partway through the quest Zogre Flesh Eaters, close to a fairy ring, making for a quick way to stock up for fletching.

* Flax can be picked in Seers' Village or Tree Gnome Stronghold and spun on nearby spinning wheels into bow strings. Noted Flax is also rewarded daily from Geoffrey in the Flax Fields south of Seer's Village based on Seer's Diary completion.

* You will naturally train Woodcutting alongside Fletching. Large groups of normal, oak, willow, maple, yew, and magic trees can be found in clusters throughout Gielinor, allowing you to gather logs in bulk before fletching them into bows. Some locations for different bow types

- Normal: Lumbridge

- Oak: West of Varrock West Bank, East of Varrock East Bank, Draynor Bank

- Willow: North of Seer's Village Bank, South of Draynor Bank, Southwest of Catherby Bank

- Maple: Seer's Village

- Yew: East of Seer's Village Church, South of Seer's Village Flax Field, Tree Gnome Stronghold South bank

- Magic: South of Seer's Village/Sorcerer's Tower, West of Tree Gnome Stronghold Bank

* Darts and arrows can be fletched while banking, travelling, or waiting during other skilling activities, making them excellent passive Fletching experience.

* Stringing Yew and Magic bows before casting High Level Alchemy significantly increases their value compared to alching unstrung bows. However, depending on your rate of leveling and available resources, you may prefer to alch the unstrung bows regardless or sell them for quicker gold.
